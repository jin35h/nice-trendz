How it works

A clear, in-store process.

  • Discuss: Explain the garment, occasion, desired fit and deadline.
  • Select: Explore fabric options and finishing details with the team.
  • Measure: Get measured in store; returning customers can ask about existing measurement records.
  • Fit & refine: Confirm fitting, alterations and any adjustments required.
  • Collect: Agree on the expected completion date directly with the store before placing the order.

Turnaround time depends on the garment, order volume and fitting requirements. Please confirm the delivery date before placing an order.
