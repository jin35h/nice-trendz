Care for your clothes

Good fabric deserves good care.

Simple habits can help preserve the look and feel of your clothes. Always follow the care label for the specific garment.

Start with the care label

Fabric composition, dye, finish and garment construction affect cleaning. Read the label before washing or ironing. If it recommends professional cleaning, follow that guidance.

Cotton and everyday shirting

  • Separate lights and darks before washing.
  • Use the temperature and cycle recommended on the care label.
  • Remove garments promptly after washing to reduce creasing.
  • Iron using a setting suitable for the fabric and finish.

Linen and linen blends

  • Expect some natural creasing; it is characteristic of linen.
  • Avoid aggressive heat unless the garment label allows it.
  • Hang carefully after washing and smooth seams while damp if permitted.

Wool and structured suiting

  • Do not assume a suit can be machine washed.
  • Air garments between wears and use a suitable hanger to maintain shape.
  • Follow the care label for cleaning and storage.

Storage

Make sure garments are clean and fully dry before storage. Give tailored pieces enough space to hang, and keep them away from direct sunlight and damp areas.

Need fabric-specific advice? Share the fabric name or composition with the NICE TRENDZ team before cleaning an unfamiliar material. This is general guidance, not a substitute for the garment's care label.
