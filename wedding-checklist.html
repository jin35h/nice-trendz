Plan ahead

Your wedding outfit, without the last-minute rush.

Use this checklist to organise style, fabric and tailoring details before the big day.

1. Understand the event

  • List the events: engagement, wedding ceremony, reception and family functions.
  • Confirm the dress code, venue and expected weather.
  • Decide whether you want classic formalwear, a suit, a blazer or traditional ethnic wear.

2. Select fabric and colours

  • Choose colours that suit the event and coordinate with other outfit pieces.
  • Compare fabric texture, weight, drape and comfort in person.
  • Ask about suitable linings, shirt fabrics and coordinated trouser options.

3. Plan tailoring early

  • Share the event date and all required outfits with the tailor.
  • Arrange measurements and discuss preferred fit.
  • Confirm fittings and completion date.
  • Leave time for adjustments and final checks.

4. Check the finished outfit

  • Try the outfit with the footwear and accessories you intend to wear.
  • Check comfort when standing, sitting and moving.
  • Confirm garments are clean, pressed and stored properly.
Start early. Tailoring schedules vary with garment complexity and store workload. Contact NICE TRENDZ with your event date to discuss timing.
