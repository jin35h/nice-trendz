A thoughtful plan

Start with the occasion. Build the outfit around it.

Whether you are preparing for a wedding, reception, engagement, festival or family celebration, the right fabric and fit can help bring your look together.

  • Discuss the event, dress code and desired style.
  • Explore suitable fabric colours, textures and finishes.
  • Plan shirts, trousers, suits, blazers, koti and ethnic outfit options.
  • Arrange in-store measurements and fittings with the team.
  • Confirm lead times early, especially for wedding dates.
