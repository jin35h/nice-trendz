Prepare for a better fitting

A little preparation.
A more considered fit.

Use this guide to prepare for an in-store tailoring conversation. Actual measurements should be taken and confirmed by the tailoring team.

Before you visit

  • Think about where you will wear the garment: work, daily use, a celebration or a wedding.
  • Bring a well-fitting garment as a reference if you have one.
  • Save inspiration images for collar, sleeve, pocket, trouser fit or silhouette.
  • Tell the team if you prefer a relaxed, regular or closer fit.
  • For a coordinated outfit, bring details about shoes or other pieces you plan to wear.

During measurement and fitting

Wear comfortable clothing suitable for measurement. Explain how you expect to move and sit in the garment, and mention any fit concerns from previous outfits. Ask which fitting stages are needed.

For wedding and event tailoring

  • Share the event date and when you need the completed outfit.
  • Discuss all pieces in the outfit together.
  • Allow time for fittings and possible adjustments. Confirm a realistic delivery date before placing the order.

Do I need to bring measurements?

No. Measurements can be taken in store. Returning customers may ask whether earlier records are available and still appropriate.

This guide does not guarantee a specific fit. The tailor should take and verify measurements for the chosen garment.
